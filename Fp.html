About Green Earth

Green Earth is an educational website designed to spread awareness about environmental protection. It explains the importance of climate change awareness, conservation of natural resources and sustainable agricultural practices.

Our mission is to encourage students and communities to adopt eco-friendly habits and protect our planet.

Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities, particularly burning fossil fuels, are a major cause of recent global warming.

Causes

Burning fossil fuels, deforestation, industrial emissions and vehicle pollution contribute to climate change.

Effects

Climate change can increase heatwaves, alter rainfall patterns, raise sea levels and harm ecosystems and agriculture.

Prevention

Save electricity, use renewable energy, plant suitable trees, reduce waste and use cleaner transport whenever possible.

Natural Resource Management

Natural resources such as water, forests and soil are essential for life. Their responsible use helps protect the environment for future generations.

Water Conservation

Repair leaking taps, avoid wasting water, collect rainwater where suitable and keep water bodies clean.

Forest Conservation

Protect forests, plant native trees, reduce paper waste and preserve wildlife habitats.

Soil Conservation

Compost organic waste, prevent soil erosion, rotate crops and maintain soil fertility.

Sustainable Agriculture

Sustainable agriculture produces food while protecting the environment, maintaining soil fertility and using water and other resources responsibly.

🌱 Organic Farming

Use compost and other suitable natural methods to support soil health and reduce dependence on synthetic agricultural inputs.

💧 Smart Irrigation

Use efficient irrigation methods, such as drip irrigation where suitable, to reduce unnecessary water use.

🌾 Crop Rotation

Growing different crops in sequence can help maintain soil health and manage certain pests and diseases.

Environmental Activities

Everyone can help protect the environment through simple actions at home, school and in the community.

🌳 Tree Plantation

Plant suitable native trees and take care of them as they grow.

♻️ Waste Management

Reduce, reuse and recycle materials. Separate waste and compost suitable organic materials.

💧 Save Water

Avoid wasting water and encourage your family and friends to conserve it.
